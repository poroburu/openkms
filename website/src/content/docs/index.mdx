## Who This Is For

openKMS is for solo operators and small teams running automated strategies,
validators, treasury bots, or backend jobs that need hot signing without hot
private keys. It assumes you can operate a hardened Linux host, keep a ceremony
mnemonic offline, and treat policy as the boundary between automation and funds.
